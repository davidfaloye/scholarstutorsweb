Do I install the app before paying?

Yes. Payment activates an existing account — the student needs to download and sign up first. Then enter that same email or phone here.

I've paid. How does the app unlock?

No code needed. Close and reopen the app, or log out and back in. Full access is there, usually under a minute after payment.

Check that the email or phone used on this page is the exact one the student signed up with in the app. If it's still locked after 10 minutes, email jamb@euritechnologies.com with the Paystack receipt and we'll fix it.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. ₦2,000 is a one-off payment for 12 months. Nothing renews unless you choose to activate again.

Can a parent or sponsor pay for me?

Yes. Tick "paying for someone else", enter the student's email or phone number, and your own email for the receipt. Anyone can pay from anywhere.

Which payment methods work?

Bank transfer, debit card, USSD, direct bank payment and OPay. You don't need a card — transfer and USSD work from any Nigerian bank account.
