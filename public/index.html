Questions

Before you pay

Do I need to install the app before paying?

Yes. Payment activates an existing account, so the student needs to download the app and sign up first. Then enter the same email or phone number on this page.

I've paid. How do I unlock the app?

You don't need a code. Close and reopen the app, or log out and back in, and full access will be there. It usually takes under a minute after payment.

My payment went through but the app is still locked.

Check that you paid with the exact email or phone number the student signed up with. If it still hasn't unlocked after 10 minutes, email jamb@euritechnologies.com with your Paystack receipt and we'll sort it out.

Will I be charged again automatically?

No. ₦2,000 is a one-off payment for 12 months. Nothing renews unless you choose to activate again.

Can a parent or sponsor pay for me?

Yes. Tick "I'm paying for someone else", enter the student's email or phone number, and the payer's email for the receipt. Anyone can pay from anywhere.

Which payment methods can I use?

Bank transfer, debit card, USSD, direct bank payment and OPay. You don't need a card: transfer and USSD work from any Nigerian bank account.

Which subjects are covered?

Use of English and the most common UTME subject combinations. More subjects are being added; the app shows the full list when you choose your subjects at sign-up.
